Production Principles

Manufacturing in Delhi, India

SM produces mechanical control cables at its manufacturing base in Delhi, supporting domestic and international automotive replacement markets.

Inner Wire & Core Assembly

Multi-wire stranded inner cables assembled for flexible mechanical load transfer across varied vehicle lever and pedal mechanisms.

Protective Outer Conduit

Coiled outer conduit casing paired with protective outer sheathing to guard against dust, moisture, and road wear during daily operation.

End Fittings & Terminals

Solid-formed nipples, barrels, eyelets, and threaded adjusters secured to match standard automotive lever and bracket mountings.

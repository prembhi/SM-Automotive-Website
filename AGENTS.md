# SM Automotive Website: Agent Instructions

## Project goal
Build a professional, fast, SEO-focused B2B website for SM, an established automotive control-cable manufacturing brand based in Delhi, India.

## Business priorities
- Display products and generate qualified business enquiries.
- Serve domestic buyers in India and export buyers in the UAE, including Dubai, and African markets.
- Support organic SEO, occasional blog publishing, and Google Ads lead generation.
- This is a product catalogue and enquiry website, not an ecommerce store.

## WordPress is the final destination
- Final platform: WordPress with Hello Elementor, Elementor Free, and compatible add-ons.
- Novamira is the intended migration/integration route. Validate its actual capabilities before choosing an implementation approach.
- Do not assume that React, Next.js, custom routing, or standalone application features will transfer automatically to Elementor.
- Design every section so it can be recreated with Elementor containers, native widgets, compatible add-ons, WordPress features, or documented minimal custom CSS/JavaScript.
- Keep content editable and avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Use semantic HTML, accessible controls, responsive CSS, and progressive enhancement where appropriate.
- Do not introduce complex frameworks or install dependencies without a clear need.

## Initial website scope
- Home
- Products and product-category presentation
- About and Manufacturing
- Contact and Request a Quote
- Reusable blog article layout and SEO-ready structure
- Include India, UAE/Dubai, and African export markets in relevant content.
- Keep the first version lean. Add dedicated market pages or advanced catalogue filters only when justified.

## Product and company accuracy
- Never invent SM's certifications, years of experience, production capacity, customer list, specifications, or export destinations.
- Competitor catalogues are research references, not proof that SM sells the same products.
- Mark missing facts as placeholders for confirmation.
- Use genuine SM product and factory photos when supplied.
- AI-generated industrial imagery may be used as illustrative artwork, but must not be presented as photographs of SM's actual factory or products.

## Design and performance
- Professional industrial B2B visual identity with clear typography and strong product photography.
- Avoid excessive animations, autoplay video, heavy effects, and decorative complexity.
- Optimise images and fonts. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Provide clear quote and contact calls to action.
- Test desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts, including overflow, long text, navigation, forms, and image loading.

## SEO and lead generation
- Use descriptive URLs, one clear primary heading per page, logical heading hierarchy, unique metadata, meaningful image alt text, and internal links.
- Design for technical SEO, schema where appropriate, sitemap readiness, and future blog content.
- Plan form success states, spam protection, privacy notice, and conversion tracking.
- Do not claim rankings or guaranteed SEO results.

## Working rules
- Inspect existing files before making changes.
- Explain the intended approach briefly before major implementation work.
- Prefer the simplest maintainable solution.
- Do not overwrite user files or make large unrelated changes.
- Keep documentation, assets, and code organised.
- Verify that the implementation works before declaring a task complete.
- Report blockers honestly and list any facts still needing confirmation.

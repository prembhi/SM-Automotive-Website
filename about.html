Company Profile

Focused Production of Automotive Control Cables

Automotive control cables are essential mechanical links that transfer driver inputs to critical vehicle components, including brakes, clutches, and throttles. SM focuses on producing dependable cable assemblies designed for reliable daily operation.

Operating from Delhi, India, SM supplies spare parts distributors, regional wholesalers, and commercial buyers with control cables built to standard fitment requirements across key two-wheeler and three-wheeler vehicle platforms.

Our manufacturing and sales teams work directly with buyers to quote on domestic wholesale orders, mixed category consignments, and containerized export shipments.

Production Overview

Cable Assembly Process

An overview of the core manufacturing and assembly stages involved in producing SM automotive control cables.

01

Inner Wire Preparation

Stranded steel inner wire ropes are measured and cut to application dimensions to provide flexible mechanical load transfer under lever pull.

02

Outer Conduit Casing

Coiled outer conduits are extruded with protective polymer sheathing to shield the inner wire against road dust, moisture, and exterior friction.

03

Terminal Fitting Assembly

Nipples, barrel ends, eyelets, and threaded adjusters are secured to the wire and casing ends to match standard automotive bracket mountings.

04

Inspection & Packaging

Assembled cables undergo visual inspection, dimensional checking, and free travel verification before being bagged and packed into master cartons.

Mechanical testing and inspection bench
Quality Inspection

Inspection & Verification Checks

Consistent quality across batches is critical for automotive spare parts distributors. Each production lot is inspected according to straightforward mechanical criteria:

  • Dimensional Checking: Verification of overall cable length, free stroke travel, and fitting dimensions against reference specifications.
  • Connection Integrity: Mechanical pull checks to confirm end terminals and crimped fittings remain securely affixed.
  • Stroke Movement: Verifying free, smooth travel of the inner wire within the outer conduit.
  • Outer Conduit Inspection: Visual check of the protective casing and end ferrules for uniform coverage.
